It does everything.

Tile windows, bind hotkeys, drive the menu bar, read battery and fans, capture the screen, talk to AI models, and hook the rest of macOS. All from tiny, hot-reloading JavaScript plugins. Built with Swift & QuickJS for maximum speed.
