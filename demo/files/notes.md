# Release notes 0.8

## New
- **Weather** shows the forecast for any city, with the next twelve hours in its menu.
- **Mini calendar** puts the month and your world clocks one click away.
- **Battery meter** shows charge, time remaining and cycle count.
- Two-line menu bar items with stoplight colors.

## Improved
- The launcher learns which results you pick and ranks them first.
- Window grid snaps to thirds and quarters.
- Plugins reload in under a second when you save them.

## Fixed
- Meetings no longer shows declined events.
- Now Playing keeps cover art when the track changes.
- The CPU graph no longer flickers on wake.

```js
macotron.menubar.status("hello", { title: "Hi", subtitle: "from 0.8" });
```
